# Milestone landscape study

`milestone-landscapes.webp` is a WebP encoding of the existing Halo art asset
`website/variants/c/assets/worlds-twelve-atlas.png`. The original remains unchanged.
The full 4 × 3 atlas is shared by seven CSS-framed environments; it is loaded once
as the explorer approaches the viewport. Each panel intentionally stays soft and
subdued under a dark mineral palette. These are atmospheric landscape studies,
not documented mineral sourcing locations.

The main page's `milestones.js` maps the selected stones to their environments.
Gradients remain available if the image cannot load. The frozen variants and
canonical twelve-month earning catalog are unaffected.
