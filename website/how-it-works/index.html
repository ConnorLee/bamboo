What is still being developed

Halo is being designed backward from a $189 retail price. NFC in every stone, selected stones, the bracelet, or no NFC are being compared. None is committed. Retention, natural materials, individual milestone presentation and companion growth require qualification. Year One completion requires a verified full year. The eligibility schedule and secure activation service are specified but not production-implemented; the existing month-index preview must not grant milestones early. Existing app screens are demo captures, not proof of a finished activation system.
