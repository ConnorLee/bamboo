Considered in every detail+

Jewelry, simply.

Brushed 316L stainless steel, twelve standardized settings and natural gemstones in removable mechanical carriers. No battery, Bluetooth, sensors or electronics in the bracelet or stones.

The intelligence stays in Halo.

Hidden NFC points belong beneath or behind the stones in the packaging. The planned app verifies the kit, milestone and eligibility before activation. Moving a stone by hand does not award a digital milestone.

Made to be kept.

One bracelet, twelve stones and one cabinet form the Halo I concept. Natural-stone sourcing, stone retention, comfort, durability and secure activation still require validation. These are design studies, not production hardware or availability announcements.

A recognizable family, over time+

Halo I begins in brushed silver. Black PVD Halo II and gold PVD Halo III explore what future years could hold. These are future concepts, not production products. A completed Halo never becomes obsolete.
