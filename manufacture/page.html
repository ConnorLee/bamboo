Development scope & evidence boundary Read before sourcing

DESIGN INTENT: one HALO system in natural brushed 316L / ivory and black brushed 316L, preferably PVD / charcoal. Shared geometry, dimensions, stone system, clasp/sizing architecture and packaging dielines. These finishes are not gendered products.

WORKING ASSUMPTION: S/M and M/L rigid chassis, each with approximately 15–20 mm underside fine adjustment. No wrist ranges are validated. The visible front approximately two-thirds remains continuous; the exact clasp and current-center indexing strategy require CAD and prototypes.

REQUIRES PROTOTYPE: passive NFC in every removable stone module, readable in final installed stainless-steel geometry. This supersedes installed suppression. Historical RF passes, dimension reviews and checklist entries remain saved but must not be read as H2 validation. The evidence states below define what supplier confirmation and physical validation require.

The current milestone must physically occupy the primary center-top/front position throughout the year while previous stones remain installed. Month 1 has one centered stone; Month 12 retains all twelve with Month 12 centered. No automatic mechanism or powered glow is assumed.

One outer presentation contains twelve branded milestone boxes/cards plus protected bracelet accommodation, with Month 01 accessible. Light and Dark share the design system; the reference dimension conflict still needs a corrected structural mockup. Review the packaging intent and fit check.

Public product narratives retain earlier NFC and stone-catalog assumptions. Reconcile them as a separate product/content decision; this H2 hardware reference does not silently change the app. The app/server owns milestone eligibility. This route is unlisted and noindex, but publicly reachable on habithalo.app; it adds no authentication. Sensitive supplier data stays in browser-local fields.
